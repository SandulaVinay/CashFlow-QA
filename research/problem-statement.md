# Problem Statement

## Working problem

Businesses use cash-flow forecasts to support liquidity decisions, but a forecast can be systematically unreliable because of data issues, payment timing assumptions, behaviour changes, structural shifts and model limitations.

## Research question

Can a vendor-agnostic system automatically assess the reliability of a company's cash-flow forecasting process using historical backtesting, data-quality signals, bias analysis and stress testing?

## Hypothesis

A forecast accompanied by independent data-quality assessment, historical backtesting, bias diagnostics and stress testing will provide more useful decision support than a forecast value alone.

## What would count as success?

A meaningful result would show that the system:

- detects material forecast weaknesses;
- quantifies forecast performance;
- identifies systematic bias or major error drivers;
- responds predictably to controlled stress scenarios; and
- remains reproducible on unseen datasets.

## Non-goal

This project is not intended to replace a CFO, accountant or financial adviser. It is a decision-support and model-validation system.
