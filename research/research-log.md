# Research Log

## 2026-09-29

### Finding 1 — Basic cash-flow forecasting is already a mature category

Commercial software already provides cash-flow forecasting and scenario functionality. The project therefore should not be positioned as a generic forecasting dashboard.

### Finding 2 — Forecast reliability is a potential gap

The working hypothesis is that businesses need stronger independent validation of forecast quality, data readiness, bias and stress behaviour.

### Finding 3 — Invoice payment prediction is already well researched

The public repository `arthurflor23/invoice-payment-prediction` provides a useful reference for multi-stage late-payment prediction and historical customer behaviour features.

### Decision

Proceed with research into **Financial Forecast Reliability & Stress Testing** rather than a generic AI cash-flow forecasting product.

### Status

Hypothesis only. No implementation performance claims yet.
